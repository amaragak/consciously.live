Unsent letter · to Mum
This letter is just for you, and isn’t meant to be sent. Writing it is the point. If you’re thinking about getting in touch with someone, give it a few days and talk it over with someone you trust first.

I never told you how much those phone calls on the drive home meant to me. I used to pretend I was busy. I wasn’t. I just didn’t know how to say I was lonely.

I’m sorry I wasn’t there more at the end. I keep replaying the last visit and wishing I had stayed the extra night.

I’m playing again. Properly. I think you’d laugh at how nervous I get.
