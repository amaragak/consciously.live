## How phrase families are classified

Recurring 3-7 word phrases are counted per style (each phrase needs 3+ videos from 3+ channels, at most 5 counted per video), grouped into families by Haiku, then classified per style from the family's rate (occurrences per 1,000 words) and prevalence (share of the style's sessions using it):

- **marker**: present (rate >= 0.2 and prevalence >= 15%) and rate >= 2.0x its rate in the other styles' videos.
- **generic**: present in at least 60% of the styles (and present here), and not a marker. Overused; keep sparing.
- **foreign**: rate <= 0.1 and prevalence <= 5% here, while another style uses it at >= 0.4 per 1k words, and not generic.
- **neutral**: everything else.
