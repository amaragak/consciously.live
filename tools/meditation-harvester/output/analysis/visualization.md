# Visualization

The Visualization sources share a spine: settle (posture, eyes closed, breaths, often tension release or a body scan), cross into an imagined place or scene, develop it through the senses, deepen or anchor, then return. Three variants appear. Place or journey scripts (steps, elevator, cave, treehouse, hammock, spaceship, forest, beach) move through a space and use descending or counting devices. Manifestation scripts (goal scene, "step into it as if already real") use questions and present-tense claiming. Light or energy scripts (golden light, chakras, heart) trace light through the body. Sleep-story and Headspace-style pieces are mostly description with a very light cue density.

Weaker fits: the forgiveness/loving-kindness teaching, the mantra and "gap" sessions, and the void/awareness pieces. The kids' scripts show a useful pattern, which is a direct "can you see it?" question followed by a pause. Several transcripts are auto-captioned with gaps, so wording is unreliable there.

Pauses: typical 2–3s between lines inside a scene. Longer silences (10–30s) come after an invitation to look, feel or enjoy. Long silent stretches of 60s up to several minutes appear in the middle or at the end (the exploration time, "I'll leave you for a while"). Sessions under 12 minutes stay short throughout. The longer ones put their long silences deep in the middle. The final tenths often end in silence or music rather than speech, so the writer should keep closing words brief.
