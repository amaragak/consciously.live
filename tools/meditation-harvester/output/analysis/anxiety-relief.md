# Anxiety relief

**What the sources share:** Nearly all open with a welcome, a posture cue, eyes closed or soft gaze, and a few deliberate breaths. Most then move to a body-based anchor (breath counting, body scan, hands or feet contact) before a longer settled stretch. Language is plain, present-tense "you", and permissive. Many name the anxious state outright and normalise it ("this will pass", "whatever you feel is okay"). Safety and "nothing to fix" reassurance recurs. Closings tend to deepen once more, return to the room, and open the eyes.

**Where they differ:**
- Pure body and breath practice versus cognitive reframing (judgments, stories, "fear isn't truthful").
- Panic talk-downs, with fast reassurance, grounding and tense-release, versus slow, silent surrender or witnessing.
- Short pause rhythm (~1.5–3s) versus a metronomic 7–8s gap per affirmation line.

**Phases:** arrival, settle breath, anchor (body or breath), the main technique (scan, count, witness, labelling), a long silent or affirmation stretch, then a gentle return.

**Poor fits:** the hypnosis session is mostly filler, with a 1,377s pause and sales content. The mantra-led session is mostly explanation. The autogenic session is mostly a single silent stretch. One is a loving-kindness / manifestation-style gratitude list. The self-compassion practice is a secondary fit. Weight these less.

**Pauses:** Spoken-cue gaps are mostly 1–5s. Silence lengthens toward the middle and end, with 20–60s stretches for settling. Very long silences (600s+) are sources' own padding, so the writer shouldn't copy them.
