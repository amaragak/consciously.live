# Sleep

**What the sources share:** Every session ends in a long silence, with no wake-up. Many end in a terminal trailing phrase ("sleep now", "rest well") followed by minutes of silence or music (up to ~1900s). The writer should end on a soft final line and let the app handle the silence.

**Phases visible:**
1. Short welcome and setup.
2. Settling breath (often counted, or noting/labelling).
3. Body release (a scan, tense-and-release, or numbered points).
4. A long imagery or story phase that descends, floats or walks toward a resting place. Some sources use narrated story only, with no body work.
5. Thinning, trailing final suggestions.

**Where they differ:**
- Hypnosis scripts: dense suggestion, long silences (10–50s), self-referential "sleep" cues.
- Story/sleepcast scripts: tight 1.5–3s pauses, concrete sensory narration, and sometimes a repeat of the whole story.
- Yoga nidra: strict numbered or paired structures.
- Affirmation-led scripts: repeated "what if" and "you deserve" lines.

**Fit issues:** The 16:04 yoga nidra and the Rod Stryker piece are structured and lean toward the awake side. The overthinking script is only a secondary fit. Several transcripts are truncated (the 20 min and 45 min ones); treat their final minutes as silence.

**Pauses:** The typical pause is ~2.5s, lengthening to 10–20s in imagery. Pauses shorten slightly in the final tenth, because lines thin out and give way to the long terminal silence. Pauses do not shrink as sessions lengthen.
