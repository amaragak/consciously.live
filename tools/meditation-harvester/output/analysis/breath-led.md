# Breath-led

**What the sources share.** Almost every session picks a breath anchor and builds around it. The technique varies:
- Ratio or count: 4-7-8, box, in 4/out 8, 6-count.
- Natural-breath awareness: noticing where the breath is felt, with return cues when the mind wanders.
- Mantra breathing: "breathing in, I… / breathing out, I…".
- Intensity rounds: deep breaths, then a hold, with timers.

Natural-breath sessions, count-led sessions and mantra sessions all use short cues followed by long silences.

**Phases.** Sessions typically divide into:
1. Arrival: posture, eyes, comfort.
2. Noticing the natural breath.
3. Shaping the breath: count, ratio, or a phrase pair.
4. A long hold or silent stretch.
5. Wandering and return, often with noting.
6. Optional widening to the body.
7. Soft return.

Count-led sessions repeat a round structure. Round cues stay stable, so the listener can predict them.

**Misfits.**
- Several secondary-fit talk-heavy items (a lecture on mortality, a vortex-affirmation script, a lecture on mindful living) are mostly teaching or manifestation, not breath-led.
- The Wim Hof rounds and the walking and telephone teachings are atypical: they're performance-style or teaching-style, not settled guidance.
- Three sessions are mostly silence after a short intro.

**Pauses.**
- The typical pause is about 2s, held through the session. This reflects short cue lines with brief gaps.
- Long silences (30s to 4 min, up to 100+ min of trailing music) appear after the technique is set, especially in the middle and later sections.
- Counting guidance often uses 1–5s gaps between numbers.
- Timer-driven sessions leave breath-hold silences of 30s to 3 min.
