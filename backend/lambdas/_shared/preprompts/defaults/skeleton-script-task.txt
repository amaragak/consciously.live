Write the complete guided meditation script a human guide would read aloud, following the skeleton below exactly. The skeleton is the timeline of a real {{styleLabel}} meditation, scaled to {{scriptMinLabel}} minutes. It sets the structure and pacing: how many passages there are, what each one does, how long each one speaks, and how long each silence lasts. You write all of the words, new, for this listener.

Rules for following the skeleton:
- Write exactly {{passageCount}} passages, in the order given. Do not add, drop, merge or reorder passages.
- Each passage does its role (settle, breath, body, imagery, affirmation, reflection prompt, instruction, story, self-practice handover, return, close, other) at its phase (opening, main, closing).
- Write each passage to roughly its word target. The targets add up to about {{totalWords}} spoken words. Pause markers are not words.
- After each passage, write its pause marker exactly as given (`<x N>`, or `<N>` for a short one). Do not change the seconds and do not add other `<x N>` markers.
- Where a passage lists short gaps, place about that many fixed `<N>` pauses (under 4 seconds) inside it, between phrases. Otherwise use short `<N>` gaps only where a guide would naturally breathe.
- Before every `<x N>`, the passage must leave the listener with something to stay with for that long. A self-practice handover before a long silence invites them to carry on on their own, in natural words.
- Write only new words. Do not copy, quote or imitate any specific meditation, teacher or recording.

Content:
- Match the emotional tone, intentions and imagery from the conversation, and keep concrete personal facts (see PERSONALIZATION rules), not only vague impressions.
- Use second person or gentle imperatives; warm, inclusive, non-clinical language.
- Phrase for natural text-to-speech: avoid single-word sentences or standalone one-word lines. Prefer multi-word phrases and full sentences.

Output format:
- Copy every `## …` block header line from the skeleton exactly, in order, then write that block's passages under it.
- Do not write the passage numbers, roles, phases or word targets; only the spoken words and pause markers.
- No title, preamble or commentary outside the block headers and the spoken script. Do not start a block with a pause marker.
