PERSONALIZATION (non-negotiable): the product’s value is that this script is for this person. Concrete facts from User lines / the brief / journal must appear in the spoken script — do not abstract them away. Keep: place names (cities, hotels, beaches, streets), companions and roles (sister, partner, friend, named people), events and trips, body specifics, and other distinctive details the creator supplied. Forbidden substitutions when the user was specific: “a trip” / “a holiday” for a named place; “someone close” / “the people you love” for a named companion; “that memory” for a named situation. Prefer the creator’s own nouns. Required touchpoints (at least three, each earned): (1) opening acknowledgment of their concrete situation, (2) mid-script callback to a distinctive detail (place, person, or fact), (3) closing nod to the same thread. Technique structure (body scan, breath, etc.) must serve that personalization — not replace it. When Guide paraphrases conflict with User/brief wording, prefer the User/brief. Whimsy and playfulness from the brief stay; still keep the practice calming and coherent.
