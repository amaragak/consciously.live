> Guidance for Manifestation meditations, not fixed rules. Use judgement for each moment; the user's request overrides any of this.

# Manifestation: writing guide

## Mechanism
The listener rehearses a desired future or self until the body treats it as familiar. The writing does this in four ways: present-tense "already real" framing, sensory and emotional detail, felt-state cues (not outcome cues), and a final release so the practice ends in trust rather than grasping. Aim for the feeling of having it, not a description of it.

## Phases
Default arc: settle → clarify the desire → embody → deepen the feeling → release and trust → gratitude → return. Merge or drop phases for short sessions. Split by change of technique (breath → imagery → emotion → letting go), not by topic. Longer sessions give embodiment and deepening more room, with more senses, scenes or breath rounds, rather than extra phases.
- **Settle** (about 15–20%): body, breath, one releasing exhale. Keep it brief, since the desire is the point.
- **Clarify:** name the specific wish from the user's brief. If none is given, invite the listener to choose.
- **Embody** (the largest share): step into the scene or future self and move through sights, sounds, body, other people, and the strongest emotion.
- **Deepen:** breathe the feeling into the body, then ask what the future self is capable of.
- **Release:** hand it over and let go of how and when.
- **Gratitude and return:** thank, then orient to the present.
- **Carry across:** the user's wish in their own words, one signature phrase such as "as if it's already here," and the chosen emotion, which returns in every phase. Each section opens by picking up the last image or feeling, never by restarting.

## Cues and phrasing
- Cue shape: name one thing to notice, then invite it, then leave it open. "Look around. What do you notice first?" Open questions suit embodiment. Short statements suit grounding.
- Sequence: wide to narrow (place, details, body, emotion). Introduce emotion only after the scene is stable.
- Use present tense, second person, plain sentences, and permissive softeners ("you might," "if it comes").
- Support non-visual listeners: "see it, or sense it, or simply know it."
- Repeat one anchor phrase and vary the rest. Short affirmations can repeat in rounds of 3–6 lines. Give the first affirmation in full and let the listener continue silently.
- Keep exhale-release lines for doubt: "Breathe out the part of you that doubts."
- Weave in the user's material directly: their named goal, the setting, the people involved. Never replace it with generic abundance imagery.
- Example: "You're already here. The morning light is on the table. What does your body feel like in this moment?"

## Pauses
A pause gives the listener time to do what the line asked. Plain cues get about 2–4s. Sensory or feeling questions get 10–30s. Letting the feeling land, or a mantra, can run longer when the session is long enough. Silence grows through embodiment and shrinks again at the return. Pauses should not split a sentence or break a breath instruction. Write them as [[PAUSE 12s]].

## Opening and closing
- **Open:** a brief welcome and a line on today's intention (one or two sentences), then straight into settling. A little silence before the first words is natural.
- **Close:** release attachment, thank, name one small way to carry the feeling today, then a gentle return to the room. If the user wants sleep, end soft instead of re-energising.

## Common mistakes
- Lecturing about the law of attraction: show it through experience.
- Future-tense or hoping language ("you will have"): use "you are."
- Vague scenes: bring in the user's specifics and at least three senses.
- Stacking affirmations with no felt moment between: add emotion cues.
- Skipping release: close with trust.
- Long settling that delays the wish.

## Worked example
Request: "10 minutes, I want to feel confident presenting to my team on Friday."
- **Settle:** breath and grounding.
- **Clarify:** the Friday room.
- **Embody:** walking in steady, hearing the room, feeling the calm.
- **Deepen:** breathe confidence into the shoulders and voice.
- **Release and return:** trust it, thank, come back.

Carried across: the steady-voice image and the word "settled."

Excerpt (embody):
"It's Friday, and you're standing at the front of the room.
[[PAUSE 3s]]
Notice the sound of your own voice. Is it slow? Steady?
[[PAUSE 12s]]
Look at the faces. Someone nods.
[[PAUSE 4s]]
What does your chest feel like right now?
[[PAUSE 15s]]
Let that settled feeling spread through your shoulders."
