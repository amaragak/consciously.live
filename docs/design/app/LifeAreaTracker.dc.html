Manifest · Life area · Tracker tab“Goals” becomes “Tracker”: a free hierarchy you write however suits the area, like a Notion tracker. Order is just how you wrote it; nothing is treated as “next”. You say what you’re on by setting Doing, or by selecting items and starting a focus session. Every row is just an item and items nest to any depth. A kind is optional (Goal, Project, Feature, Task, or none); only Goals get the “How I’ll get there” plan and the dream link. Status is optional; progress only rolls up from tickable children, otherwise a parent shows “n items”. Shown with the Consciously area.
Outline (default)The whole tree with columns: expand any row in place. “Add anything…” at the bottom; Tab / Shift+Tab indent. The view you pick is remembered per area.

As many levels as you likeAny item can have children, and those children their own, with no limit. On hover a row shows a small + in its left gutter (add an item inside), and on the right a zoom icon (parents only) and ⋯. Keyboard: Enter adds a sibling, Tab makes it a child of the row above, Shift+Tab moves it up a level. Dragging moves rows; sliding left or right while dragging changes the level, and the gold drop bar shows exactly where and at what level it lands. Indentation stops growing after 4 levels; deeper rows keep the same indent and show an L5, L6… tag. Progress rolls up through every level.

Select, then focusNo more fixed “next 3”. Hover any row and a round select dot appears in the left gutter (separate from the square done box).
