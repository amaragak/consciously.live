Waking up

Alex wakes before the alarm, rested, in a house full of light at the edge of the hills. He sits for an hour before anyone else is up, because it is the best part of his day. Thoughts come and go and none of them grab him. Whatever happens later, he starts from here.

Consciously

By nine he is at his desk. Consciously has passed a million people now, and every morning there are messages: someone who slept properly for the first time in months, someone who finally made the call to their brother. He reads a few with his coffee.

The team is small, brilliant and kind, and the company runs without him holding it up. He works on whatever interests him most and lets the rest go.

The music

Afternoons are for music. The studio is at the bottom of the garden, and there is always something half-finished on the desk. The EP did better than anyone expected; the new songs are better again.

Once a month he plays live, and somewhere in the second chorus the room starts singing his words back to him. He has stopped being surprised by it, but never stopped being grateful.

Body, friends and money

He trains four times a week, the way other people brush their teeth. He is strong, lean and at ease in his body, and he looks it. Some evenings he runs up the hill behind the house just to watch the sun go down.

Friends come for long dinners that run past midnight. He is generous with his time and his money, because there is more than enough of both. When he travels, it is because he wants to, and he always comes home gladly.

Who he is now

When things go wrong, as they sometimes do, he notices how little it shakes him. He is awake, in the oldest sense of the word, and everything that happens is happening for him.

He knows exactly how lucky he is. Every night he falls asleep a little more certain that this is only the beginning.
