What has Music become?

I play most weeks. It started with the open mic at the Snug, then the small rooms in Manchester, and now people come because it’s me on the bill. At the Deaf Institute the room sang the last chorus back to me.

The app is the other half of my week. Millions of people open it before bed, and every day someone writes to say it changed their life. I still read as many as I can.
