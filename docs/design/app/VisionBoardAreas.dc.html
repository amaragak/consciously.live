Vision board · grouped by life area, with wordsThe board is your vision, not a file grid: each life area shows its dream line (from your vision) beside a small collage of its images. Favourites lead each collage and show their caption. The library moves into a drawer, so nothing is shown twice. View full screen plays the board one image at a time with its words.
BoardAreas in vision order. The lead image at 4:3 with its caption; the rest at 3:4 beside it, caption on hover (Morning runs). Tiles keep sensible shapes and never stretch to fill. An area with no images shows a Create image tile for it. Images without an area sit at the end, full width, with no heading. Area name opens the area.
