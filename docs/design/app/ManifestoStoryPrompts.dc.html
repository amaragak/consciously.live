Manifesto · StoryOne continuous piece of long-form writing with a listening meter towards ten minutes (~1,400 words). Prompts cover who you are and how you live and push for delusional optimism (Go bigger), with switches to include your life areas and values; Talk it out for dictation, and follow-up questions to keep going. Write in the first person if easier; Make it my story does an AI pass (third person by name, grammar, flow) keeping your words; Narrate makes the audio.

Everything worked out, and then some. What does your life look like now?

I’m calm, in a way people notice. I’m generous with my time, I say what I mean, and I finish what I start. Creativity isn’t something I squeeze in; it’s how I spend my days.

How do you feel when you wake up?52 words
What has Music become?

I play most weeks. It started with the open mic at the Snug, then the small rooms in Manchester, and now people come because it’s me on the bill. At the Deaf Institute the room sang the last chorus back to me.

The app is the other half of my week. Millions of people open it before bed, and every day someone writes to say it changed their life. I still read as many as I can.
