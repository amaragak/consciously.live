Reflection· Made 12 Oct 2026, 04:09Finding Ground When Your Dreams Aren’t Met with Support A gentle practice for processing the hurt of sharing something meaningful (your business idea) and not receiving the support you hoped for. Through breath and body awareness, you’ll make space for disappointment and reconnect with your capacity to hold your dreams.
Beatrice|Echoes of the Ancients|★★★★★Your rating

Script812 wordsFollow alongCopy
Arriving
0:00Find a position that feels steady. Let your eyes close, or soften your gaze toward the floor.
0:24There’s nothing you need to fix right now. You’re just here, with whatever came with you.
pause · 6s
Meeting the hurt
1:10You shared something that mattered to you, and it wasn’t met the way you hoped. Notice where that lands in your body.
1:48Perhaps a tightness in the chest, or a heaviness in the stomach. Let it be there. Breathe toward it, not away from it.
pause · 8s
2:40Disappointment is a sign of how much you care. It doesn’t have to mean the dream was wrong.
Holding the dream
3:30Picture the idea itself, separate from anyone’s reaction to it. What is it like to hold it in your own hands?
Made from“Felt flat after pitching the business idea to family last night.”
